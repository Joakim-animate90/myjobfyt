**MJF-055**

## Problem

Applications cannot be reviewed, authorised or tracked, and the authorisation screen is where informed consent actually happens.

## Business impact

If the candidate cannot see exactly what will be sent, their consent is not informed.

## Technical impact

An application list and Kanban board, a per-application timeline, the authorisation screen showing the real artefacts, and standing-authorisation setup with its cap, expiry and plain-language summary.

## Proposed solution

Build the authorisation screen to display the actual CV version, rendered cover letter and email body before the approve action. Standing authorisation shows a plain-language summary of exactly what it permits, with a visible revoke control. The timeline shows what was sent.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The authorisation screen shows the exact artefacts that will be sent
- [ ] Standing authorisation setup states its cap, expiry and minimum score in plain language
- [ ] Revoke is visible and takes effect immediately
- [ ] The Kanban board reflects real statuses including questions_pending and outcome_unknown
- [ ] outcome_unknown is shown honestly as 'we could not confirm', never as success or failure
- [ ] The timeline shows every event with its timestamp

## Testing requirements

A test asserting the approve action is unavailable until artefacts have loaded. A test asserting outcome_unknown renders as uncertainty.
