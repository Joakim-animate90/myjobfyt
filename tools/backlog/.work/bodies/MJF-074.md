**MJF-074**

## Problem

Metrics and traces are emitted but nothing collects, stores or alerts on them.

## Business impact

Without alerting, failures are discovered by candidates rather than by the team.

## Technical impact

Collector deployment, Prometheus with recording and alerting rules, Grafana dashboards, Loki with log shipping, and alert routing.

## Proposed solution

Deploy the stack via Helm. Build dashboards for the pipeline funnel, consumer lag, AI spend, submission outcomes and per-source health. Alert on dead-letter depth above zero, consumer lag, submission failure rate, AI error rate, p99 latency and database saturation.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Traces span the full asynchronous chain as one trace
- [ ] Dashboards exist for the funnel, queues, AI spend and source health
- [ ] Dead-letter depth above zero alerts immediately
- [ ] Logs are queryable by request_id, correlation_id and trace_id
- [ ] No alert fires on a healthy system for 24 hours

## Testing requirements

Induce each alert condition and confirm it fires and routes. Verify a correlation id resolves across traces, logs and metrics.
