**MJF-026**

## Problem

The same job appears on many boards. Collapsing them wrongly merges distinct jobs; not collapsing them floods the candidate with duplicates.

## Business impact

Duplicate matches make the product feel broken. A wrongly merged pair silently hides a real opportunity.

## Technical impact

A deterministic fingerprint over normalised company, normalised title, geo key and a shingled description hash. Exact collision attaches a source attribution; near matches are linked, never merged.

## Proposed solution

Implement fingerprinting and the dedupe path. On exact fingerprint match, add a job_posting_sources row to the existing posting. On near match above the similarity threshold, insert both and link them as near_duplicate — merging two distinct jobs is unrecoverable, a link is cheap and reversible.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The same posting from three boards yields one job_postings row with three attributions
- [ ] Near-duplicates are linked with a similarity score, not merged
- [ ] The fingerprint is deterministic for identical input
- [ ] jobs.normalised is published only when content actually changed
- [ ] The UI can show 'also on Indeed, LinkedIn' from the attributions

## Testing requirements

A corpus of known duplicate and near-duplicate pairs with expected outcomes, including hard negatives that must not collapse.
