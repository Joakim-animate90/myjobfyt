**MJF-023**

## Problem

Aggregators give breadth with a weak apply path. ATS board APIs give clean structured data AND a real, lawful application path, which is the only place fully server-side submission is legitimate.

## Business impact

This tier is what makes genuine application automation possible rather than just job discovery.

## Technical impact

Per-company board endpoints, company allowlist seeded by ATS detection, and the submission endpoints each provider documents.

## Proposed solution

Implement the three board adapters plus ATS-provider detection for company careers pages. Maintain a company allowlist. These are the sources where the submitter in MJF-043 can act server-side.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each adapter lists a company's public postings
- [ ] ATS provider and job id are recorded on the posting so submission can find its way back
- [ ] Capabilities declare supports_submission
- [ ] The company allowlist is configurable and auditable

## Testing requirements

Fixture conformance tests per provider. ATS-detection tests against recorded careers-page HTML.
