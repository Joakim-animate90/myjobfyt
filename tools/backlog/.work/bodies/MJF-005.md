**MJF-005**

## Problem

Extracted text is unusable for matching until it is structured into experience, education, skills and certifications.

## Business impact

Match quality depends entirely on extraction quality. A wrongly parsed CV produces irrelevant matches and the candidate blames the product.

## Technical impact

Schema-constrained LLM extraction into cv_parsed_data, with a confidence score and a character offset per field so the UI can show provenance.

## Proposed solution

Define a strict extraction schema. Chunk long CVs. Persist per-field confidence and offsets in the confidence column. Surface low-confidence fields for candidate review rather than trusting them silently. Candidate corrections are recorded in corrected_fields and are never overwritten by a re-parse.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Experience, education, skills, certifications and languages are extracted into the documented schema
- [ ] Every field carries a confidence score and a character offset into the source text
- [ ] Fields below the confidence threshold are flagged for review
- [ ] A re-parse never overwrites a field listed in corrected_fields
- [ ] total_experience_months and detected_seniority are derived and stored
- [ ] An injection string in the CV does not alter the extraction instructions

## Testing requirements

A labelled corpus of at least 20 real-shaped CVs with expected extractions, asserting a minimum field-level accuracy. Adversarial CVs containing prompt-injection text.
