**MJF-027**

## Problem

Postings disappear upstream. Applying to a dead posting wastes a candidate's daily quota and looks incompetent.

## Business impact

Quota is scarce on the free tier. Spending it on an expired job is a direct harm.

## Technical impact

A nightly re-check of postings seen in the last 30 days, publishing jobs.expired and cascading to matches and applications.

## Proposed solution

Scheduler job re-checking liveness per source, batched and rate-limited. Set status=expired and cascade match and application status.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A posting removed upstream becomes expired within 24 hours
- [ ] Matches and un-submitted applications cascade to expired
- [ ] A submitted application is NOT retroactively changed
- [ ] The re-check respects per-source rate limits

## Testing requirements

Integration test: posting disappears from a fixture source, assert the cascade and that submitted applications are untouched.
