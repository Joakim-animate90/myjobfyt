**MJF-045**

## Problem

Status mutated in place destroys history, and the history is what makes the audit trail and the analytics trustworthy.

## Business impact

A candidate must be able to see exactly what happened to each application, and the analytics numbers must reconcile with it.

## Technical impact

Append-only application_events, with status derived from the log rather than written directly.

## Proposed solution

Implement the event log and derive status from it. Every transition appends an event with its actor, payload and correlation id. The timeline view reconstructs from the log.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every status change appends an event; no status is written without one
- [ ] The full history is reconstructible from the log
- [ ] The timeline shows the artefacts that were actually sent
- [ ] Analytics derived from the log reconcile exactly with the per-application timeline

## Testing requirements

A test replaying an event log and asserting the derived status matches at every step. A reconciliation test between analytics aggregates and per-application histories.
