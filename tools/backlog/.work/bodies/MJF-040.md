**MJF-040**

## Problem

Each posting supports a different application channel and the choice must be explainable to both the candidate and an administrator.

## Business impact

A candidate needs to know why a job requires manual application rather than being applied to automatically.

## Technical impact

Ordered selection — ats_api, public_form, authorised_email, extension, manual — driven by the posting, the loop's automation settings and entitlements, with the choice and its reason persisted.

## Proposed solution

Implement method selection as a pure, ordered function so it is exhaustively testable, persisting both the method and the human-readable reason on the application.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Selection is deterministic and the reason is persisted
- [ ] A posting requiring an authenticated session routes to the extension channel, never to server-side automation
- [ ] Disabled automation settings route to manual rather than failing
- [ ] The reason is shown to the candidate

## Testing requirements

An exhaustive table test over posting shapes and automation settings, asserting the chosen method and reason for each.
