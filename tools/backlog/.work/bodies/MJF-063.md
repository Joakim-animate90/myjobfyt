**MJF-063**

## Problem

Funnel analytics computed independently of the event log will disagree with the per-application history, and then neither is trusted.

## Business impact

A funnel number that contradicts the detail view destroys confidence in all reporting.

## Technical impact

Nightly rollups aggregating application_events into per-user, per-loop and per-CV summaries, plus live counters for today.

## Proposed solution

Derive every aggregate from the event log, so the dashboard and the timeline cannot disagree. Compute per-CV reply and interview rates with confidence intervals and an explicit minimum-sample gate.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every aggregate derives from application_events
- [ ] Dashboard totals reconcile exactly with per-application histories
- [ ] Confidence intervals and sample sizes are computed and exposed
- [ ] Below the minimum sample, no verdict is produced
- [ ] Rollups are idempotent and re-runnable

## Testing requirements

A reconciliation test comparing aggregates against a replay of the event log. An idempotency test re-running a rollup.
