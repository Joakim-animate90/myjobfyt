**MJF-033**

## Problem

Applying repeatedly to the same company embarrasses the candidate and annoys the employer.

## Business impact

Reputational harm to the candidate is a direct product harm.

## Technical impact

A configurable per-candidate, per-company suppression window, defaulting to the observed six months, surfaced with the prior contact date.

## Proposed solution

Implement using company_contact_cooldowns. Surface the suppression as already_applied or email_already_sent WITH the prior date, so the candidate understands rather than suspects a bug.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A second application to the same company inside the window is suppressed
- [ ] The suppression is surfaced with the prior contact date and method
- [ ] The window is configurable
- [ ] Cooldown is per candidate, independent of other candidates

## Testing requirements

Tests at the window boundary, and a tenant test asserting one candidate's cooldown does not affect another's.
