**MJF-062**

## Problem

Candidates cannot tell whether their applications were read or replied to.

## Business impact

Reply detection drives the status pipeline and the analytics funnel.

## Technical impact

A disclosed, disableable tracking pixel; per-application reply addresses; inbound ingestion threaded against applications.

## Proposed solution

Implement tracking and reply ingestion. Inbound mail is untrusted: attachments are scanned, HTML sanitised, and no inbound content ever reaches a prompt instruction position. Open tracking is presented as indicative because image blocking makes it unreliable.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Opens are recorded and the pixel can be disabled by the candidate
- [ ] Replies are threaded to the correct application
- [ ] Inbound attachments are AV-scanned and HTML is sanitised
- [ ] No inbound content reaches a prompt instruction position
- [ ] The UI labels open data as indicative

## Testing requirements

Injection tests on inbound email content. Threading tests with malformed reply addresses.
