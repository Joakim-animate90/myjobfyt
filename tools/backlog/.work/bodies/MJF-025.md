**MJF-025**

## Problem

Raw postings from 20 boards have incompatible shapes. Without normalisation, deduplication and location scoring are impossible.

## Business impact

Deduplication depends on normalised identity. A candidate seeing the same job five times loses trust immediately.

## Technical impact

Company name normalisation, location geocoding to a structured form with a geo key, salary parsing to currency/period/range, employment type and seniority mapping, language detection.

## Proposed solution

Implement the normalise consumer. Normalise company identity so 'Acme, Inc.' and 'Acme Inc' resolve to one company. Geocode locations; on geocoder failure persist the raw string and backfill later rather than failing the posting.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Company variants resolve to one companies row
- [ ] Locations produce a stable geo_key for matching
- [ ] Salaries are normalised to min/max/currency/period with is_estimate recorded
- [ ] Language is detected and stored
- [ ] Geocoder unavailability does not lose the posting
- [ ] Schema drift quarantines the payload rather than corrupting the dataset

## Testing requirements

Table tests for company, location and salary normalisation with real-world messy inputs. A quarantine test with a malformed payload.
