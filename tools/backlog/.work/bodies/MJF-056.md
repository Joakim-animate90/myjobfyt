**MJF-056**

## Problem

Screening questions block submissions, and the funnel is invisible without an analytics view.

## Business impact

Pending questions silently stall applications. Funnel visibility is what lets a candidate improve.

## Technical impact

The questions queue with sensitive-question marking, the funnel dashboard, per-CV comparison with confidence intervals, and the notification centre.

## Proposed solution

Build the queue showing which questions need the candidate specifically and why. The analytics dashboard shows the funnel from the event log, labels small samples, and declines to name a winner below the minimum sample. Open tracking is labelled as indicative, not fact.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Sensitive questions are marked as requiring the candidate, with the reason
- [ ] The funnel reconciles with the per-application timelines
- [ ] Small samples are labelled and no verdict is shown below the threshold
- [ ] Open rates are presented as indicative, with the reason stated
- [ ] Notification preferences work per category

## Testing requirements

A test asserting no winner is declared below the sample threshold. A test asserting sensitive questions cannot be AI-answered from the UI.
