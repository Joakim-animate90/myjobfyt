**MJF-051**

## Problem

A candidate cannot sign up, verify, sign in or complete onboarding.

## Business impact

The first five minutes decide whether a candidate ever reaches a match.

## Technical impact

Sign-up, sign-in, email verification, and a resumable onboarding wizard covering profile, CV upload and first loop.

## Proposed solution

Build the flows against the live API. Accessible forms with Zod validation and inline server errors. The onboarding wizard is resumable, with completeness shown as progress and the specific missing fields named.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Sign-up, verification and sign-in all work against the running API
- [ ] Form validation matches the server's rules and shows field-level errors
- [ ] Onboarding is resumable after a reload
- [ ] The password field shows the real policy, not invented composition rules
- [ ] Keyboard navigation and screen-reader labels are correct throughout

## Testing requirements

End-to-end journey test from sign-up through to a configured loop. Accessibility tests on every form.
