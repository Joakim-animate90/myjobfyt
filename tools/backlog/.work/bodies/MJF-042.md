**MJF-042**

## Problem

No application may be submitted without recorded candidate authorisation. This is the product's integrity boundary.

## Business impact

Applying on someone's behalf without their consent is the worst thing this product could do. It must be structurally impossible, not merely unlikely.

## Technical impact

Per-application approval, plus a standing authorisation scoped to one loop with a daily cap, a minimum score and an expiry, capturing a snapshot of what was agreed. Immutable records, fully audited.

## Proposed solution

Implement both paths writing immutable application_authorisations rows. The existing applications_sent_requires_authorisation constraint is the backstop. Re-check validity, cap and the loop pause flag immediately before every dispatch.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] No application reaches a sent state without a valid authorisation
- [ ] A standing authorisation has a daily cap, a minimum score and a mandatory expiry
- [ ] Every authorisation stores a snapshot of exactly what was agreed
- [ ] Revocation takes effect before the next send
- [ ] Every grant and revocation is audited
- [ ] A submission completing just after a pause is recorded as authorised_before_pause

## Testing requirements

A dedicated security test suite attempting to submit without authorisation through every code path. Cap-exhaustion, expiry and revocation-race tests. This suite is a release gate.
