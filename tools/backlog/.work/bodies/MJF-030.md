**MJF-030**

## Problem

Most postings must be rejected before scoring, and a candidate who sees no matches currently has no way to find out why.

## Business impact

'Why am I getting no matches?' is the single largest support burden in this product category. Retained rejections turn it into a self-service screen.

## Technical impact

Ordered hard filters — excluded company, excluded keyword, required keyword missing, location, work mode, language, employment type, seniority floor — each persisting a categorised rejection.

## Proposed solution

Implement the filter layer, persisting match_rejections with the category and detail. Required keywords use OR semantics (at least one must appear), matching the documented behaviour. Company exclusion matches normalised identity, not raw strings.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each filter rejects correctly and records its category
- [ ] Required keywords are OR semantics; excluded keywords are any-match
- [ ] Company exclusion matches 'Acme, Inc.' and 'Acme Inc' alike
- [ ] A rejected posting remains directly applicable by the candidate
- [ ] Rejections are browsable and filterable by category

## Testing requirements

A table test per filter including boundary cases. A test asserting a rejected posting can still be applied to.
